# ORB Trade Operations

ORB is a custom bilingual trade-operations platform for international back-to-back coffee trading. It is designed around projects, purchase and sales contracts, supplier and customer commercial invoices, shipments, documents, payments, collections, and project-level operational profitability.

## Product principles

- Custom web application; no Odoo dependency.
- Arabic RTL and English LTR from the product foundation.
- Project-centered transaction, shipment, document, and reporting workflows.
- Approval, audit, permission, evidence, and currency-aware controls.
- Material Design 3 with ORB branding.

## Development status

ORB is under active development. Repository contents and task progress do not by themselves represent production readiness. Production use requires verified tests, independent review, controlled deployment, security checks, backups, monitoring, and owner acceptance.
